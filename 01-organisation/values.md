# Values

Pokapu is guided by the 10 Principles of Burning Man, which provide the foundation for our organisational culture and approach to supporting volunteer-led events.

We seek to embody these principles in how we operate, while helping the communities and events we support to uphold them in their own ways.

We recognise these principles are descriptive rather than prescriptive, and encourage their thoughtful interpretation and application. 

## The 10 Principles of Burning Man

### Civic Responsibility

We recognise our responsibility to the communities we serve and the wider public. We support responsible event organisation, promote safety and wellbeing, and operate with integrity, accountability, and respect for applicable laws.

### Communal Effort

We believe collaboration makes us stronger. We develop our platform openly, encouraging people to contribute, share knowledge, and build on each other's work. Together, we create tools and resources that benefit the wider community.

### Decommodification

We place people and community ahead of commercial interests. By making our platform open-source, we seek to keep essential tools accessible, prevent unnecessary dependence on commercial providers, and ensure community benefit remains our priority.

### Gifting

We value the generosity of people who freely contribute their time, skills, knowledge, and resources. We share our work openly so that others can use, improve, and build upon it, without expecting anything in return.

### Immediacy

We believe meaningful human connection and direct experience are at the heart of community. Our tools and processes should remove barriers to participation and support real-world experiences, rather than becoming ends in themselves.

### Leaving No Trace

We respect the environment and recognise our responsibility to minimise our impact. We encourage sustainable practices in our own operations and help event organisers protect and care for the places their communities gather.

### Participation

We believe communities thrive when people are actively involved. We encourage people to contribute to our platform, share ideas, shape its development, and take ownership of the tools they use.

### Radical Inclusion

We welcome people from all backgrounds and experiences. We strive to make our organisation, platform, and resources accessible and inclusive, removing unnecessary barriers and fostering an environment where everyone is treated with dignity and respect.

### Radical Self-expression

We celebrate individuality, creativity, and diverse perspectives. We empower people and communities to adapt our tools to their own needs, explore new ideas, and develop their own approaches, while respecting the rights and freedoms of others.

### Radical Self-reliance

We believe in empowering people and communities to develop their own capabilities. By making our platform open-source, we enable others to understand, adapt, and operate their own solutions, reducing unnecessary dependence on Pokapu.
